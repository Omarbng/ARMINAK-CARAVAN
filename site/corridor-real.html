Trade Corridors & Waypoints

The live figure draws one line through all five points in sequence, which reads as a single journey. The page's own copy says something different — three producing regions consolidated through the Gulf, then delivered out. This version draws that structure, and puts every waypoint at its true position: three origination lanes converging on Jebel Ali, one delivery lane leaving it. Grid lines are 10° of latitude and longitude.
